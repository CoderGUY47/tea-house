Heritage & Ethos

Rooted in slow living.
Grounded in Sylhet soil.

Founded in 2012, The Tea House was born from an unyielding belief: in a bustling capital that moves at dizzying speed, tea is the gentlest way to pause and reclaim your breath.

DHAKA

The Sreemangal Foothills

Between morning mist
and monsoon rain.

Our story begins in the lush undulations of Maulvibazar and Sylhet. In high-canopy biodynamic tea gardens where ancient trees shade tender young tea bushes, our partner farmers harvest only two leaves and a bud at daybreak.

Unlike mass-commercial commodity teas that are pulverized into indistinguishable dust, every leaf in our collection is carefully withered, sun-basked, and gently rolled to preserve its essential botanical oils and natural sweetness.

Principles

How we honour
the leaf.

Every decision we make honours traditional farming and the quiet dignity of deliberate craft.

Whole Leaf Integrity

We never use CTC dust or factory sweepings. We exclusively source whole unbroken leaves that unfurl naturally in hot water, releasing layered complexities.

Fair Farmer Partnerships

We pay premium prices directly to smallholder family growers in Sylhet and Panchagarh, ensuring living wages and community healthcare support.

Natural Botanicals Only

Our blends use genuine whole ingredients - organic lemongrass, wild chamomile blossoms, crushed cardamom pods, and sun-dried verbena.
