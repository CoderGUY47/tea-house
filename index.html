Our philosophy

Tea is not a
thing to rush.

We started Tea-Buffe with a simple belief: a good cup should create a pause. We source characterful leaves and natural botanicals, then blend them with restraint so the ingredient - not the process - stays at the centre.

No loud flavours. No unnecessary ingredients. Just tea that rewards the extra minute.

The tea ritual

A better cup starts
with slowing down.

Three mindful steps. No elaborate ceremony required — just fresh water, patience, and leaves given room to breathe.

Choose your leaf

Match the tea to your mood, not the clock. Bright, bold or botanical.

Give it time

Use fresh water and let the leaves open naturally. Good things unfold.

Take the pause

Put the phone down. Notice the aroma. Let the first sip arrive slowly.

Philosophy

There is always time for tea.
There is not always time for hurry.

- Tea-Buffe, Dhaka
